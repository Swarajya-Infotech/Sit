3. Engineering for Indian Realities: Offline-First Architecture

A common reason mobile apps fail in industrial and rural India is fragile network dependence. When a delivery driver enters a basement parking lot or a warehouse supervisor conducts an audit inside a sheet-metal plant in Bhosari, cellular signals drop to zero.

How Swarajya Infotech's Offline Sync Engine Works:

  1. Local-First Writes: Every user action (generating a sales quotation, scanning a QR code, capturing a customer signature) is instantly saved to an encrypted local SQLite database in milliseconds.
  2. Queueing Worker: A background service monitors network connectivity status without interrupting user interaction.
  3. Atomic Batch Sync: When connectivity returns, changes are batched and posted to our central Node.js/PostgreSQL cloud API.
  4. Conflict Resolution: Timestamps and vector clocks automatically resolve simultaneous edits, ensuring zero data loss.
