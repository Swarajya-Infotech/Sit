Corporate Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. Scope and Commitment

SWARAJYA INFOTECH ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"), headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, India, is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all personal data, proprietary enterprise architectures, and confidential business metrics shared with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and safeguard data acquired via our website (swarajyainfotech.in), client software discovery sessions, demo environments, and engineering consultation services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with SWARAJYA INFOTECH, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Direct Communications & Inquiries: Full name, corporate email address, business telephone/WhatsApp number, company name, project requirements, and budget expectations submitted via contact or demo forms.
  • Technical & Architectural Specifications: Technical workflows, database schemas, ERP specifications, and system integration parameters provided under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
  • Automated Telemetry & Website Analytics: Browser type, operating system, IP address, referral sources, and pages visited collected through privacy-focused web metrics to optimize site performance and security.

3. Legal Grounds for Processing

We process your data strictly under recognized lawful bases in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) and global standards including GDPR:

  • Contractual Performance: To draft software architecture proposals, calculate custom pricing, execute Master Services Agreements (MSAs), and deliver engineering milestones.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our digital infrastructure against DDoS attacks, cyber threats, and fraudulent form submissions.
  • Explicit Consent: Where you provide consent to receive technical architecture whitepapers or product release updates.

4. Client IP & Zero-Data-Selling Guarantee

We never sell, monetize, rent, or trade client personal or enterprise data to third-party brokers or advertisers. All proprietary source code, internal algorithms, customer lists, and database backups developed for our clients belong 100% to the client upon milestone delivery, and are maintained in private, encrypted repositories accessible only by authorized engineering leads.

5. Data Security & Storage Standards

All communications across swarajyainfotech.in are secured through modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Internal data storage complies with ISO 27001-aligned controls, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and encrypted cloud vaults (AES-256).

6. Data Retention & Your Legal Rights

We retain business inquiry records only as long as necessary to service your project requirements or fulfill statutory tax and legal obligations under Indian law. Under applicable data protection statutes, you possess the following rights:

  • Right to access and review any personal data held by SWARAJYA INFOTECH.
  • Right to rectify inaccurate or obsolete corporate information.
  • Right to request complete erasure of your contact details from our records.
  • Right to revoke consent at any time without penalty.
