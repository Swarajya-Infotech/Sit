Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers about software ownership, development process, and long-term maintenance.

Custom software development services entail designing, engineering, testing, and deploying bespoke software applications built specifically around your organization's unique operational workflows. A business should build custom software when off-the-shelf SaaS solutions either force disruptive workflow workarounds, incur exorbitant per-seat monthly license fees, or cannot integrate with existing shop-floor hardware, legacy ERPs, or proprietary databases.

In Pune and Maharashtra, bespoke software costs depend on scope and architecture. A streamlined internal workflow tool or MVP typically ranges from ₹45,000 to ₹95,000. Comprehensive multi-department web applications, client portals, and CRM engines range from ₹95,000 to ₹2,50,000. Full-scale enterprise industrial ERPs with IoT/hardware sync and custom mobile apps range from ₹2,50,000 to ₹6,00,000+ as a one-time capital investment with 100% full source code ownership.

At Swarajya Infotech, you retain 100% intellectual property (IP) and source code ownership upon project completion. We hand over the complete Git repository, Docker containers, database schemas, and architectural documentation. There are no proprietary lock-ins or licensing royalties.

A standard Minimum Viable Product (MVP) or departmental workflow automation tool takes 3 to 6 weeks. Mid-sized custom business applications take 6 to 12 weeks, while complex industrial enterprise platforms with hardware telemetry require 12 to 20 weeks, delivered in iterative agile milestones with live staging previews.
