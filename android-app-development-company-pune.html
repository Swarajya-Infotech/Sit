3. Architecture: Native Kotlin vs Flutter for Pune Enterprises

When clients consult us at our Pune office, the first question is whether to build pure Native Kotlin or cross-platform Flutter. Here is how we evaluate your use case:

When to Choose Native Kotlin
  • Industrial IoT apps interfacing via raw Bluetooth Serial Port Profile (SPP).
  • Heavy background geolocation tracking running 24/7 on delivery personnel phones.
  • Custom Android OS firmware deployments on dedicated industrial POS tablets.
When to Choose Flutter (Cross-Platform)
  • Customer-facing e-commerce, booking, and on-demand service applications.
  • When you need both Android and iOS apps from a single codebase, saving 40% budget.
  • Clean Material Design 3 UI with 60fps/120fps fluid micro-animations.
