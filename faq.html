SWARAJYA INFOTECH is a premier enterprise software development company based in Pune, Maharashtra. We specialize in building mission-critical custom architectures, web applications, mobile apps, and AI-driven industrial telemetry for businesses looking to scale seamlessly.

We serve a wide range of industries including Education (School Management Systems), Healthcare (Hospital Management Systems), Hospitality (Hotel & Resort Solutions), E-commerce, Retail, and Manufacturing (Industrial Automation).

Ready-made software is a generic solution built for mass markets. It is fast to deploy but often forces businesses to change their workflows to fit the software. Custom software is engineered specifically for your unique business operations. It scales with your growth, ensures you own your data, and provides a competitive advantage without recurring licensing fees per user.

The cost of custom software development varies significantly based on project scope, complexity, required integrations, and technology stack. Typical enterprise projects range from ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,00,000+. We provide a detailed architectural blueprint and cost estimation after an initial discovery consultation.

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software centralizes your business data into a single unified system. This eliminates manual data entry errors, automates repetitive administrative tasks, tracks inventory in real-time, and provides actionable analytics for management, ultimately increasing operational efficiency and reducing costs.
